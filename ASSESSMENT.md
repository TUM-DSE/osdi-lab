During this course, students will be assessed on project milestones, the final report, and the presentation.

## Grade Calculation

The final grade is the weighted average of individual and group milestones, final report, and final presentation.

 * **Individual Grades (25%)**
   - 10% Milestone Presentations (M0, M1)
   - 10% Milestone Integration Tests (M7)
   - 5% Report (M7)
 * **Group Grades (75%)**
   - 25% Milestone Presentations (M2, M3, M4, M5, M6)
   - 30% Milestone Integration Tests (M1--M6)
   - 10% Report (M1--M6)
   - 10% Class Presentation

The course staff reserves the right to conduct additional assessments, interviews, or scale the grade if a team member's contributions are deemed disproportionately below or above.

## Milestones

Milestones build upon each other. To help with prioritization of the milestone tasks, we distinguish between:

 - Base tasks: minimum requirement. Later milestones depend on this functionality.
 - Target tasks: target functionality for a more complete implementation.
 - Extra Challenges: Optional. Not required for full marks.


> [!IMPORTANT]
> To pass the course, participants and teams must successfully complete the base tasks for each milestone.

Extra challenges only earn bonus points iff and only iff the base and target tasks are completed successfully.


## Deadlines and Late Submissions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The deadlines for the course milestones are **hard**. Missing a deadline counts as late submission.

Late submissions will be graded at 75% (one-week delay) and 50% (two-week delay).

A late submission cannot earn points for extra challenges.


## Milestone Presentations

Participants present each milestone to a member of the course staff.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We expect each participant to demonstrate that they have implemented the required functionality and be able to answer questions about their solution.
> Failure to answer questions or demonstrate the required functionality will result in a lower milestone grade.

For group milestones, each member must be able to explain the group's implementation and design decisions.

At a minimum, participants and teams must successfully and convincingly demonstrate that their implementation meets the base requirements for the milestone.
